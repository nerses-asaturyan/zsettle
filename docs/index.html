Trade on Zcash without trusting anyone in the middle.

zsettle moves an item and its payment in one shielded transaction that both sides sign. Either both happen, or nothing does. No escrow, no custodian, no marketplace database deciding who owns what.

Try the live demo Download

The demo runs on the Zcash testnet: coins are free, and you get some when you sign up. The server holds the demo wallets; for real ZEC, run the app on your own computer.

Atomic

The seller signs the item's half, the buyer signs the payment's half. Nothing is valid until both halves are in one transaction.

Private

Buyer and seller stay shielded. The seller sees only the price and the time of the sale, never the buyer's address or balance.

Provable

An item is a real shielded note. Anyone with the seller's viewing key can trace its history from the chain, without asking anyone.

How a trade works

  1. The seller lists an item.Items are shielded notes. The listing is signed with the item's key, so buyers know it is genuine.
  2. The buyer builds the whole trade.Both halves, the item moving to the buyer and the ZEC moving to the seller, proven with zero-knowledge proofs on the buyer's device.
  3. The seller checks and signs only its half.The seller's shop verifies the proof and the price, then signs the item half. It never sees the buyer's keys.
  4. The buyer signs and broadcasts.One transaction, settled by Zcash consensus. If the item was sold elsewhere first, the trade is invalid and the buyer's ZEC never moves.
